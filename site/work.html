FAQs

Questions, answered.

What is StayUp Social?

StayUp Social is a social media marketing agency that also builds websites and AI agents. We manage Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, run Meta and Google ads, edit reels, create content and build brands for startups, local businesses, doctors and founders. StayUp Social is a Crescive Ventures company, founded by Harshit Sharma.

Where are you based, and do you work with clients in other cities?

We’re based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, and work with brands across India and abroad, including clients in the UK and the US. Calls, approvals and reports all happen online, so your location is never a barrier.

Do you only work with big brands?

No. We work with startups, local businesses, doctors, founders and growing brands across the world. If you’re serious about growing, we’re a fit.

Will I own my website, content and ad accounts?

Yes, always. Your website, social accounts, ad accounts and everything we create for you belong to you, from day one.

How will I know it’s working?

You get a clear monthly report on reach, leads, enquiries and ad spend, in plain language. You also get a strategy call to review the numbers and plan what’s next.

How fast can we get started?

Most projects begin within a week of our first call. We start with a free audit, agree on a plan and goals, and then get to work.

Is my business information safe with you?

Completely. Your data, logins and business details stay confidential, and we’re happy to sign an NDA before we begin.

Questions about our services

What does a social media marketing agency do?

A social media marketing agency plans, creates and posts content for your brand, manages your community and runs paid ads, then reports on what’s working. At StayUp Social that covers Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn management, reels and content creation, Meta and Google ads, and a clear monthly report.

How much does social media management cost?

It depends on the platforms, how many posts and reels you need each month, and your ad budget. After a free 45-minute call we share a clear, fixed quote with no hidden costs, so you know exactly what you’re paying for before we start.

Can you grow my Instagram?

Yes. We plan a content calendar around your audience, write hooks and scripts, edit reels for watch time and post consistently. Every month we track reach, saves, followers and enquiries, and change what isn’t working.

Do you offer personal branding for doctors, founders and experts?

Yes, it’s a core part of what we do. We help founders, doctors and experts turn what they know into content people follow, from scripts and shoots to editing and posting on Instagram and LinkedIn.

Do you run Meta and Google ads?

Yes. We run Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and LinkedIn ads for leads, sales and bookings, with tested creatives, weekly tuning and reports in plain language.

Do you build websites and handle SEO?

Yes. We design and build fast, mobile-first websites that are set up for search from day one: clear pages, page titles, structured data and a sitemap. We also help with hosting and connecting your domain.

What is an AI agent, and how can it help my business?

An AI agent is software that handles routine work for you, like answering enquiries on WhatsApp or your website, qualifying leads and booking calls, at any hour. We train it on your business and connect it to the tools you already use.
